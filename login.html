Multi-Device Cloud Setup

Aethera connects to Google Firebase Auth and Cloud Firestore to allow logging in from any phone, laptop, or browser anywhere in the world.

How multi-device login works:

Accounts created in Aethera are securely stored in the cloud. When you open Aethera on another device (e.g. your phone), entering your credentials authenticates and restores all your saved workflows and models in real-time.
